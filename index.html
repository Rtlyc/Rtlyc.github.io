About Me

Welcome to my academic portfolio! I'm a researcher focusing on the intersection of robotics, artificial intelligence, and game design. My work explores how robotic systems can learn from gaming environments and how cat-like agility can be implemented in robot movement patterns.

When I'm not in the lab, you can find me playing strategic board games, designing pixel art, or spending time with my cats who often inspire my research on movement algorithms.
