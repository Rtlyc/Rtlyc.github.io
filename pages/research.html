Robot Learning

My research in robotic learning focuses on developing algorithms that allow robots to learn from experience, similar to how players improve in games. I'm particularly interested in reinforcement learning techniques that enable robots to adapt to new environments.

Current Projects

  • Adaptive Robot Navigation

    Teaching robots to navigate complex environments using game-inspired pathfinding algorithms.

  • Multi-Agent Robot Cooperation

    Developing systems where multiple robots can work together to solve tasks, inspired by multiplayer game mechanics.

Feline Biomechanics

Cats have remarkable agility, balance, and efficiency in movement. My research explores how these natural capabilities can be translated into robotic systems.

Current Projects

  • Dynamic Equilibrium in Quadruped Robots

    Studying how cats maintain balance during rapid direction changes and implementing these principles in robotic systems.

  • Energy-Efficient Movement Patterns

    Analyzing the energy conservation techniques in feline locomotion for more efficient robot movement.

Game AI

Game environments provide rich testbeds for AI research. My work in this area focuses on developing intelligent agents that can learn and adapt in complex game worlds.

Current Projects

  • Procedural Level Generation

    Creating algorithms that can design balanced and engaging game levels automatically.

  • Adaptive Difficulty Systems

    Developing AI that can adjust game difficulty based on player performance to maintain optimal engagement.
